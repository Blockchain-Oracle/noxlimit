Density strategy

High, but earned by proportion rather than boxes. There are only four containers on the desktop screen; everything else is separated by a 1px rule. The rail card is its own unit bar — the indigo/amber ground washes in from each edge at the YES/NO proportion, so a scan down the rail reads as six differently-weighted units before you read a single number.

Typography

Archivo on its width axis: wdth 118 for display and the wordmark, 100 for UI. IBM Plex Mono carries every number, always tabular. Prose gets the sans, evidence gets the mono — so a value that came off-chain never sits in the same voice as a label we wrote.

Color logic

Reclaim's clay #D97757 and green #19C37D, used exactly the way Reclaim itself uses them — as two categories filling one proportional bar. Their hero splits a bar into Claude Code and Codex footprint; this splits a unit into YES and NO. Same device, same palette.

Because the pair now reads green/warm, every instance is doubled with a filled vs. outlined glyph so nobody reads NO as an error state.

Chart treatment

The plot plane is divided by the strike into outcome territory: the region above the strike carries a 5% indigo wash and is labelled YES resolves, below is amber and NO resolves. The line itself stays ink, because in Underlying mode the series is USD — not an outcome price. That single rule is how the design keeps the two prices from ever being confused.

The private field

A withheld fill: the one panel in the product whose ground is hatched instead of filled, and the only field with a full-ink border. Beneath it, a 0–1 scale pins your limit against the live pool quote and draws the distance between them as a dashed gap — which is literally what the order is waiting on. Not a lock icon, not a glow.

Lifecycle & status

Status is a named string plus a form change, never a color: the check budget is eight discrete cells that fill as they're spent, so Monitoring exhausted is a picture of an empty budget rather than a red pill. Publication pending removes the hatch from the private panel — the ground turns solid, because the value is no longer withheld.

Motion signature

Proportional, not decorative: bars interpolate their width when a quote updates, so a moving market is visible as movement of the unit itself. Card selection cross-fades the two washes. Under prefers-reduced-motion widths snap and nothing else moves.

Accessibility

Every proportion has its number adjacent; the unit rail is an img role with a text summary. Outcome side is carried by a filled vs. outlined glyph in addition to hue. Ink on ground measures 15.2:1 light and 18:1 dark. Reclaim's hues are kept exactly on the dark ground (8.4:1 and 6.4:1); on warm paper they fall to 2.1:1 and 2.8:1, so light mode uses derived twins #0E7A5E and #A8482A at 4.7:1 and 5.2:1. Countdowns always show the absolute UTC time beside them.

Fit, complexity & risk

Why NoxLimit: the product's one unmistakable fact is that outcome shares are fractions of a unit. No exchange UI is built on that, because on an exchange price is unbounded.

Complexity: moderate — the unit bar and 0–1 scale are two reusable primitives that cover most of the screen.

Risk: the wash on rail cards can get noisy at six cards; needs a hard opacity ceiling. And green/clay for YES/NO inherits the profit-loss reading a neutral pair avoids — the glyph redundancy carries it, but it is the one place this palette costs something.

A measuring instrument, not a dashboard. YES and NO carry no colour at all — they're a solid mark above the axis and a hollow mark below it. The only chromatic element on the entire screen is the field you're keeping private.

Density strategy

Maximum density, made legible by structure rather than whitespace. Panels are separated by a 1px chassis seam and marked with registration crops, so the eye reads the screen as an assembly of instruments. The rail is a numbered rack — 01–06 is real information (your position in the filtered stream), not decorative numbering.

Typography

JetBrains Mono is promoted from caption face to body face — it sets labels, values, timestamps and the quote ladder. Instrument Sans is used only where prose actually needs to be read: the market question and explanatory notes. The inversion is the point: in this direction, measured data is the default voice and English is the exception.

Color logic

This direction spends the Reclaim palette differently: it takes the ink and paper grounds and one single hue — clay #D97757. Green never appears. YES and NO have no hue whatsoever, just a filled square above the axis and a hollow square below.

Clay is reserved for exactly two things: the private field and the distance-to-strike measurement. One colour on a 1440×900 screen, marking the one value you are protecting.

Chart treatment

An engineering drawing. Axis labels sit outside the plot on a hairline frame; the strike is a chain-dashed line (long-short-short) as in a section cut; a red dimension bracket measures the 127.87 the price must travel to cross it. No area fill, no glow — the plot is a measurement, and everything drawn on it is a quantity you could read off with a rule.

The private field

A recessed module inside a red dimension bracket, with the bracket label set vertically along the left edge exactly as a drawing would annotate a toleranced region. It's the only saturated area on a 1440×900 screen, so it holds attention without motion, glow, or a lock glyph.

Lifecycle & status

Statuses are typeset as instrument readouts — uppercase mono with a leading square, never a coloured pill. The check budget is drawn as a ruler: eight graduations with major marks at 1, 4 and 8, and spent checks thicken to a heavy tick. Monitoring exhausted is a ruler with every graduation consumed.

Motion signature

Almost none, deliberately. Values transition by digit-roll on the mono numerals; the crosshair readout tracks the pointer. Selection extends the registration crops from 8px to the full panel edge — a mechanical click rather than a fade. Nothing pulses, nothing glows.

Accessibility

The strongest of the three on this axis: because outcome side is encoded in fill and position, the interface is fully readable in monochrome and under any colour-vision deficiency. Reclaim's green/clay pair therefore carries no meaning it could lose. Ink hits 15.2:1; clay runs #A8482A on paper (5.2:1) and #D97757 on ink (6.4:1). The chart carries a text summary naming source, as-of time, and distance to strike.

Fit, complexity & risk

Why NoxLimit: the product's honesty rules — no fabricated candles, no depth, everything labelled with source and block — are engineering-drawing values. This direction makes the constraint the aesthetic.

Complexity: highest. Crop marks, dimension brackets and outside-plot axes need real care to stay aligned across breakpoints.

Risk: can read as cold or unfinished to a first-time trader, and mono-as-body slows prose reading. Mitigation is the sans on the question line and generous line-height in notes.

Privacy expressed as light behaviour rather than iconography. The public market is lit; the market you're not looking at is dimmed; and your private instruction is the one region whose luminance is inverted — the single dark slab in light mode, the single lit slab in dark mode.

Density strategy

The lowest density of the three, and the only one that treats attention as the scarce resource. One market is on a lit stage; the other five sit in the rail at 60% opacity like frames on a strip. That maps the adopted Discover grammar — one market at a time — onto the desktop terminal instead of contradicting it.

Typography

Instrument Serif sets the market question at 38px, because the question genuinely is a sentence you read, not a ticker you scan — this is the only direction that treats it that way. Manrope handles UI, IBM Plex Mono every number. The serif never touches a value.

Color logic

Colour is scarce on purpose. Reclaim's green and clay are the only hues on the page, reserved for the two outcome series and paired with ▲ / ▼ so nothing depends on hue. Reclaim's twin radial washes — clay from the top-left, green from the top-right — sit under the whole shell at 5–14%, which is where the brand actually lives in this direction.

Chart treatment

Stripped to the observation itself: no grid, no frame. A dotted strike rule labelled in the serif, a warm luminous wash under the line that fades to nothing, and one filled dot at the current value. The area fill is light, not a hue — it's the same gesture as the lit stage, one level quieter.

The private field

The signature. The slab's luminance is inverted against whatever theme you're in: in light mode it's the only dark region on the page; in dark mode it's the only lit one. Flip the theme toggle and watch it stay the exception both times. Nothing else in the product does that, so the eye finds it instantly and it never needs a padlock.

Lifecycle & status

Named text plus form. The check budget is eight lamps that go dark as they're spent, so a vigil that has burned down is literally visible — Monitoring exhausted is eight unlit lamps and an action. At Publication pending the slab loses its inversion and joins the lit page: the value is no longer withheld, and the interface stops pretending otherwise.

Motion signature

Light, and only light. Selecting a rail card raises it from 60% to full over 180ms while the previous card falls back. The resting-order indicator breathes on a 3.4s cycle. Under prefers-reduced-motion the opacity change is instant and the breath is replaced by a static lamp — no information lives in the motion.

Accessibility

The one direction where dimming is load-bearing, so it needs the most care: unfocused rail cards are held at 60% opacity, measured at 6.7:1, and keyboard focus lights a card fully rather than adding a ring alone. Outcome side always carries ▲/▼. The inverted slab is a contrast gain, not a loss — 17:1 in light and 15.2:1 in dark.

Fit, complexity & risk

Why NoxLimit: the product's real emotional beat is that something of yours keeps watch while you're gone. No other direction has a place to put that feeling.

Complexity: low-to-moderate — mostly opacity and two token flips.

Risk: the highest of the three. Lower density means the quote ladder and portfolio strip get less room, and dimming inactive markets is a real usability bet on a screen where people compare. If Abu wants a dense terminal first and delight second, this is the wrong pick.

All three use identical sample data from the product surface map — the same six-card catalog, the same 25.00 Test USDC order at a 0.44 private maximum implying 56.818182 minimum shares, the same real calcBuyAmount ladder. Nothing is fabricated: no candles, no volume, no bids or asks, no depth chart, no placeholder market. The 48-point BTC/USD series is labelled design sample data everywhere it appears.

Palette: all three now share the Reclaim identity from getreclaim.xyz — ink #08080A, paper #F4F2EC, clay #D97757, green #19C37D, and the paired clay/green radial washes. Dark mode uses Reclaim's exact tokens. Light mode uses derived darker twins, because the source hues measure 2.1:1 and 2.8:1 on warm paper and this interface is built from 12px numerals. Typography stays per-direction.
